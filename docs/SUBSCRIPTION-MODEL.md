# Aether: support subscriptions

Aether is open-source software. It is free to use under its open-source licence, and that does not change.

Teams that want supported releases and defined response targets can buy an annual **support subscription** from Zyvor.

| Plan | What you get | Coverage |
|------|--------------|----------|
| Community | The software, community documentation and public issue tracking | No contracted response target |
| Standard | Deployment and upgrade guidance, defined supported configuration | Business hours |
| Supported | Standard plus priority queue and incident triage | Business hours, faster response |
| Enterprise | Supported plus a named technical contact and 24x7 critical intake | One-hour initial response target for critical issues |

Support is priced on the one **Zyvor Support** meter: per node per year, covering Aether and the other Apache-2.0 Zyvor infrastructure on that node. It is never stacked per product, so a node that already holds Zyvor Support does not pay again. List price per node per year: **Supported** USD 600 worldwide (excl. tax) or **INR 59,999 in India (incl. 18% GST)**, minimum 5 nodes (USD 3,000 or INR 299,995). **Supported Plus** (adds 24x7 Severity 1 and 2, quarterly reviews and upgrade assistance) is USD 1,200 or INR 119,999 in India, minimum 5 nodes (USD 6,000 or INR 599,995). Volume: 256-499 nodes -10%, 500-999 -15%, 1,000+ -20%; multi-year: 2 years -5%, 3 years -10%; volume and multi-year together are capped at 25%. Minimums are annual contract values and apply after discounts. The software stays free; the purchase is support.

Prices: INR incl. 18% GST in India; US dollars excl. tax elsewhere (VAT, GST or sales tax added where applicable). Pay online by UPI, card or netbanking where a buy page exists; international cards work (charged in rupees); or request a US-dollar invoice by bank transfer. Buy or get a quote: [zyvor.dev/buy](https://zyvor.dev/buy). Custom pricing: contact [sales@zyvor.dev](mailto:sales@zyvor.dev). A 30-day trial key is available for production licences; Aether itself stays free under Apache-2.0.

## Terms

- Annual prepaid subscription is the default, with a 12-month minimum. Multi-year prepay is available.
- Minimums apply after any discounts.
- Implementation, migration, training and managed operations are separate services.
- At expiry, updates, downloads and support end. Open-source rights, where they apply, continue under the licence.
- Response targets need a contracted scope and are not uptime guarantees.
- Existing signed agreements keep their agreed terms.
