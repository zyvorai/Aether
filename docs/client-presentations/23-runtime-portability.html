Flagship Story

Deploy once. Move workloads across containers, Kubernetes, VMs, and bare metal without rewriting infrastructure.

One spec · Four runtimes · Explain · Migrate

Three proof points

What buyers should remember in 30 seconds.

1. One spec, four runtimes

Validate once with `aether validate`. Deploy to Podman, Kubernetes, KubeVirt, or Metal3.

2. Production migration

16 pairs, blue-green default, rollback on failure, 30s drain cap. Honest limits on volumes/DNS.

3. Explainable placement

`aether decide --explain` and dashboard Intent Debugger show +/- per runtime.

Enterprise HA

Postgres state, Redis sessions, OIDC — see Deployment Topologies deck #02.
