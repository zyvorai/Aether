Scheduling Strategies

Aether's scheduler evaluates every runtime against your workload requirements and selects the optimal placement based on the active strategy.

⚖

Balanced (Default)

Weighs both cost and performance equally. Allocates 30% scoring weight to cost efficiency and 30% to resource headroom, producing well-rounded placements that avoid extremes.

💰

Cost Optimized

Minimizes estimated daily cost by favoring runtimes with the lowest cost-per-CPU and cost-per-GB rates. Podman typically wins for small workloads at $0.50/CPU/day vs Metal3 at $3.00/CPU/day.

⚡

Performance Optimized

Maximizes resource headroom by favoring runtimes with the lowest utilization after placement. Spreads workloads across runtimes to prevent contention and noisy-neighbor effects.

📦

Bin Packing

Packs workloads tightly onto the fewest runtimes possible. Maximizes utilization on active runtimes, allowing unused runtimes to be powered down for infrastructure savings.

# Schedule with a specific strategy
$ aether schedule my-app --strategy cost
$ aether schedule my-app --strategy performance
$ aether schedule my-app --strategy bin-packing
$ aether schedule my-app --strategy balanced
The scheduler scores each candidate runtime from 0.0 to 1.0, combining strategy weight (60%), preferred runtime bonus (20%), affinity score (15%), and capacity headroom (10%).

Constraint-Based Filtering

🎯

Runtime Constraints

RequireRuntime and ExcludeRuntime constraints hard-filter the candidate list before scoring begins. Co-locate and anti-affinity constraints link placements between workloads.

💻

Hardware Constraints

RequireGpu filters to runtimes with available GPUs. RequireBareMetal restricts to Metal3 only. MaxCostPerDay caps the estimated daily cost per placement.

# Schedule with constraints
$ aether schedule gpu-trainer \
    --cpu 8 --memory 32768 --gpu 2 \
    --constraint require:gpu \
    --constraint zone:us-east-1a \
    --preferred-runtime kubevirt

Utilization Metrics

📈

CPU Utilization

Calculated as (total_cpu - available_cpu) / total_cpu. Tracked per-runtime and used by the Performance strategy to spread workloads and by the Bin Packing strategy to consolidate them.

📊

Memory Utilization

Calculated as (total_memory - available_memory) / total_memory. Combined with CPU utilization to produce a composite utilization score that drives placement decisions.

💰

Cost Tracking

Each runtime tracks cost-per-CPU-day and cost-per-GB-day rates. The scheduler estimates daily cost for each placement candidate and surfaces total runtime cost in the utilization summary.

⚠

Capacity Warnings

When a placement pushes utilization above 85%, the scheduler emits a warning. Approaching the max workload limit also triggers a warning to prevent capacity exhaustion.

Optimization Categories

Category Trigger Action
CAPACITY CPU utilization > 90% Scale up runtime or migrate workloads out
COST CPU utilization < 10% with active workloads Consolidate to cheaper runtime
COST Small workload on expensive runtime Move workloads using <4 CPUs off Metal3
BALANCE Utilization deviates >30% from average Redistribute workloads across runtimes

Placement History

Every scheduling decision is recorded as a Placement record with workload name, selected runtime, reserved CPU, reserved memory, and RFC 3339 timestamp. When a workload is deleted, release() frees the reserved capacity back to the runtime. This history enables capacity planning, chargeback reporting, and audit trail compliance.
